# Moving Average

Sampling frequency is 1000 Hz, and we apply a $N$-tap moving average filter. (e.g., $N=20$)

The following document is generated by AI:

The filter is

$$
y[n] = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} x[n-k].
$$

Its transfer function is

$$
H(z) = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{k=0}^{N-1} z^{-k}
= \frac{1}{N}\frac{1-z^{-N}}{1-z^{-1}}.
$$

The frequency response is

$$
H\left(e^{j\omega}\right)
= \frac{1}{N}e^{-j\omega\frac{N-1}{2}}
\frac{\sin\left(N\frac{\omega}{2}\right)}{\sin\left(\omega/2\right)}.
$$

Therefore,

$$
\left|H\left(e^{j\omega}\right)\right|
= \frac{1}{N}\left|\frac{\sin\left(\frac{N\omega}{2}\right)}{\sin\left(\omega/2\right)}\right|,
$$

and the linear phase term is

$$
\phi(\omega) \approx -\omega\frac{N-1}{2}
\quad\left(\text{or }\phi(f) = -180\frac{N-1}{1000}f\;[\mathrm{deg}]\right).
$$

The first null appears at 50 Hz = (1000/20), and nulls repeat every 50 Hz.
